Studio Stats Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Studio Stats is a private tool a video studio uses to read the performance numbers of its own channels. This policy explains what data it accesses and how that data is used.

Who uses it

Only the studio, for the YouTube channel and Facebook Page it owns. It is not available to the public, and only the owner of those accounts can authorize it.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube: read-only access to the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports and basic channel and video information (scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly): views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, likes, comments and subscribers gained, per video and per day.
  • Facebook: read-only access to the Page's own insights (reach, plays, reactions and follows per post).

It does not access private messages, contacts, payment details, or any other person's account.

How the data is used

The numbers are kept in the studio's internal records and used only to measure and improve its own videos. They are never sold, rented, shared or used for advertising, and never combined with data about other people.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Studio Stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Data is kept on the studio's own computer behind account access controls and is never published. Access tokens are stored privately and used only to read the reports described above.

Retention and deletion

Numbers are kept as long as they help measure the studio's videos. The account owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook's Business Integrations settings. After that the tool can no longer read any data, and stored numbers are deleted on the owner's request.
