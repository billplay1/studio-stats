Studio Stats

Studio Stats is a private reporting tool that a video studio uses for its own YouTube channel and its own Facebook page.

What it does

With the account owner's permission, it reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained per video) and the owner's own Facebook Page insights, and keeps those numbers in the studio's internal records so the team can see which videos work and make the next ones better.

What it does not do

  • It never posts, uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything.
  • It asks for read-only access only.
  • It is used only for the studio's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and never accesses anyone else's account.
  • It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
